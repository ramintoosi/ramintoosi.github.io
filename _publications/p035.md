<h3> Abstract </h3>
Reinforcement Learning (RL) has become a central paradigm for sequential decision-making, yet practical deployment remains challenged by sample inefficiency, instability, and scalability in complex environments. This survey reviews RL foundations and algorithms—including model-free value-based and policy-based methods, actor-critic architectures, model-based RL, Deep RL, Multi-Agent RL, and Multi-Agent Deep RL—and connects them to widely used software libraries. Building on this algorithmic landscape, we examine case studies in non-terrestrial wireless networks (NTNs), highlighting how RL methods address decision-making under mobility, coverage, resource allocation, and reliability constraints. We also discuss open challenges around safety, interpretability, sample efficiency, and computational efficiency.
