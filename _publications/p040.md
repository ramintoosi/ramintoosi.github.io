<h3> Abstract </h3>
This paper studies visual imagination through topological data analysis of neural dynamics, focusing on the role of alpha-band phase. Using topological descriptors of oscillatory activity, we investigate how alpha-band phase organization relates to imagined visual content and characterize structure that is not readily captured by conventional spectral power analyses alone.
