A POSIX-style shell implemented in Rust for the CodeCrafters "Build Your Own Shell" challenge.

Covers a readline-style REPL, builtins, PATH-based external programs, quoting, I/O redirection, pipelines, background jobs, tab completion, and history with persistence.
